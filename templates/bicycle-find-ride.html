Find a Ride

Find cycling opportunities offered by independent clubs, bike shops, and event organizers in Central Oregon and nearby areas.

COBC provides links to these organizations as a community information resource. COBC does not organize, sponsor, supervise, endorse, or insure the rides listed here.

Check with the organizer before you ride. Ride information can change. Verify the current schedule, meeting location, registration, waiver and insurance requirements, pace, distance, terrain, equipment, and cancellation policy directly with the responsible organization.

Choosing an Appropriate Ride

Questions to Ask Before Joining

Compare the advertised pace and distance with your current ability. Ask whether the ride is no-drop, how often the group regroups, what terrain and equipment are expected, and whether membership or advance registration is required.

If you are uncertain, contact the organizer before arriving. Start with a shorter or slower ride until you understand the group’s riding practices.
