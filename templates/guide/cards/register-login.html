🔐 Registering and Logging In

Create and access your TFOL account

Guests can browse public content. Registered members can create and submit stories, upload images, and use their personal content tools.

Basic steps

  1. Click Register.
  2. Complete the registration form and choose a password.
  3. Confirm that you are at least 18 and accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
  4. Check your email and open the verification link.
  5. Return to TFOL, click Log In, and sign in.

What happens

Your account becomes available after your email address is verified. If you do not receive the message, check your Spam or Junk folder. TFOL can also send you a new verification email.

Why this matters

Registration gives you access to TFOL member features while email verification helps protect your account and the TFOL community.
