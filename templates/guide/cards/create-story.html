✍️ Creating a Story

Write, save, and request review

Once you are logged in, you can create a story, save it as a private draft, and request a review when it is ready to be shared.

Basic steps

  1. Go to the story creation page.
  2. Enter a title and add your story text.
  3. Upload an image if desired.
  4. Select Save Draft to continue working later.
  5. Select Request Review when the story is ready.

What happens

A draft is not public. After you request a review, a TFOL administrator can review the story and publish it.

Why this matters

The Draft/Review process gives you time to develop your story while helping ensure that published TFOL content is ready to share.
