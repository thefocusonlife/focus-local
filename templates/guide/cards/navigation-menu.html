🗂️ The Navigation Menu

Explore the main TFOL sections

The navigation menu helps you move between the major parts of TFOL. On a phone, you may need to tap the menu icon first.

What you may see

  • Get Focused — browse TFOL’s topic websites
  • Focus — explore content about focus and clarity
  • About — learn about TFOL
  • Help — find assistance with using TFOL

Try this

  1. Click About.
  2. Explore one of the available topics.
  3. Use Back to return.

What happens

Each link opens a different main section of TFOL.

Why this matters

Understanding the main navigation makes TFOL easier to explore.
