Discover how personal memories become stories, why the four featured stories are arranged the way they are, and how to begin your own.,

Stories Worth Saving is the front door to The Focus On Life. It is a place to begin with a memory, a moment, a family story, or a small piece of life that deserves to be remembered.

The first two featured stories are simple examples of how a personal memory can become a story. The last two show how one story can grow into a collection, a series, or a larger theme over time.

On a larger screen, such as a computer or tablet, you will see more of the story grid at one time. This makes it easier to browse, read, and get familiar with the site. A phone works well too, but the cards stack vertically so there is more scrolling.

For your first visit, a computer or tablet may be the easiest way to explore. For adding photos, however, a phone may be best because that is where most pictures are already saved.

When you are ready, choose Write Your Story. If you do not already have an account, the site will guide you through the simple registration process and then return you to your story.

Start small. A few sentences about one memory is enough. You can always add more later.
