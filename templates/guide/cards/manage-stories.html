📝 Editing and Deleting Stories

Manage your drafts and published work

The Stories page lets you review the status of your stories, continue working on drafts, revise published stories, or delete content you no longer want.

Basic steps

  1. Log in and click Admin.
  2. Click Stories.
  3. Use All, Review Requested, Private, or Public to filter the list.
  4. Select edit to revise one of your stories.
  5. Save it as a draft or select Request Review when the revision is ready.

Deleting a story

Select delete beside the story. TFOL displays a confirmation page where you can cancel or confirm the deletion.

Important

Editing and saving a public story makes it private again. Request another review when the revised version is ready to be published.

Deleting a story also deletes its comments and uploaded image. This action cannot be undone.

Why this matters

The Stories page gives you one place to manage your drafts, review requests, published stories, and revisions.
