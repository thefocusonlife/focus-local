📚 Exploring a Story Menu

Browse related stories together

TFOL members organize related stories into menus. A menu may represent a subject, project, collection, or continuing series.

Try this

  1. Open a story.
  2. Look for the words Posted In.
  3. Click the menu name shown beside it.

What happens

TFOL opens that menu and displays other stories organized under the same subject.

Why this matters

Menus let you follow one subject through a member’s collection without having to search the entire site.
