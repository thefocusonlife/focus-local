🧭 The Top Bar

Account links and quick navigation

The top bar provides quick access to important TFOL pages and tools. If you see Guest, you are not currently logged in.

What you may see

Guest / Log In / Register | Sort / Refresh / Back / Stories / Websites / Guide

The available links may change depending on the page you are viewing and whether you are logged in.

Try this

  1. Look at the top bar near the top of the page.
  2. Click Stories to open Stories Worth Saving.
  3. Use Websites to explore other TFOL websites.
  4. Use Back to return to the previous TFOL page.

What happens

Each link opens a frequently used TFOL page or performs a navigation action. Account links also let guests register or log in.

Why this matters

The top bar provides a consistent way to reach Stories Worth Saving, other TFOL websites, the Guide, and common account and page controls.
