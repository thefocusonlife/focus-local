🗃️ Organizing Your Stories

Create menus for your subjects and projects

Menus are one of TFOL’s main organizing tools. Each menu groups stories under a subject, project, collection, or continuing series.

Basic steps

  1. Log in and click Admin.
  2. Click Menus.
  3. Select Add new menu.
  4. Enter a name and description for the menu.
  5. Choose its position and default story order.
  6. Save the menu.
  7. Select that menu when creating or editing a story.

What happens

The menu becomes one of the subjects available for organizing your stories. Visitors can open the menu to browse its related published stories.

Why this matters

Menus turn separate stories into an organized collection. They help you develop subjects over time and make your content easier for others to explore.
